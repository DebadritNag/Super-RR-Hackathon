# Problem statement: requirements summary and conflicts

Source: `Round 1 Problem Statement _ SupeRR Selector Season 2.docx.pdf` (2 pages). Full extracted text:
`problem_statement.txt` (the PDF stores one word per line; the extractor rejoins them). This summary
was written by reading that text, not generated by keyword matching.

## What the PDF requires

1. A data-driven system that measures how batters and bowlers perform in high-pressure match
   situations **compared with their usual performance levels**, and ranks the players who deliver
   most consistently when it matters.
2. "Pressure" is for the participant to define. Suggested ingredients: required run rate, wickets in
   hand, balls remaining, stage of the innings, how close the contest is, or a combination, plus any
   other match-context signal. The definition must be explained and shown to separate pressure from
   ordinary situations. The method must work for **both batting and bowling**.
3. Data: historical **IPL and T20I data from 2022 onwards**: ball-by-ball CSV plus competitions,
   teams, players, matches, playing XIs and competition-team-player mappings.
4. Submission has two required parts:
   - **Player rankings:** two ordered lists, **top 10 batters** and **top 10 bowlers** (ranks 1 to 10,
     no repeated player within a list), chosen through dropdowns on Unstop. Selections must match
     the rankings explained in the report.
   - **Methodology report** (document upload) covering: pressure definition; performance measurement
     (batting and bowling metrics, player baselines, rating/scoring logic); fair comparisons (sample
     sizes, player eligibility, different match contexts, use of IPL and T20I data); evidence for the
     top 10 (results, comparisons, tables or charts); reliability and limitations (consistency across
     matches, where the model is less certain). It must be transparent enough to reproduce.
5. Rankings must be supported by analysis of the supplied data, not reputation.
6. Optional (does not affect evaluation): photos, screenshots or a 30 to 60 second video.

## Not stated in the PDF

- No numeric minimum sample size, eligibility threshold, IPL/T20I weights, or scoring formula.
- No page or word limit, report format, deadline, or evaluation rubric.
- No statement about whether data outside the supplied files (the Cricsheet JSON) may be used.
- The dropdown player list on Unstop is not available to us, so spelling or identity differences
  between our `player_name` values and the dropdown cannot be checked.
